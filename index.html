Selected Research

AAAI 2027 · SubmittedFirst Author

PACE: Propagation-Aware Collaborative Correction for One-Shot Personalized Federated Graph Learning

Federated Learning · Graph Neural Networks · Collaborative Learning

PACE keeps each receiver's complete local model while cross-client knowledge arrives only as a compact low-rank correction. Clients upload a Rank-6 update carrier and propagation-moment sketches; the server forms a propagation-aware RegMean consensus, and each receiver uses one-dimensional convex NLL calibration to selectively adopt or reject external knowledge without extra communication.
